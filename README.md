# Album 96 - Do Not Cease to Hear Instruction

Eight bilingual songs from selected lines in Proverbs 19:11–23:35. Do not stop hearing the words that give knowledge. The Lord’s favour is dew on the grass. The spirit of man is the Lord’s lamp. He turns the heart. Buy the truth and do not sell it. There is a latter end, and the hope shall not be cut off. Wine bites at the last like a serpent. Tamil original plus English cinematic companion.
